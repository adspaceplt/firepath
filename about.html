About Firepath

Exclusive to Johor. Focused on fire protection.

Firepath Sdn. Bhd. is the exclusive Tyco / Johnson Controls distributor for Johor, based in Kulai.

The range covers four areas: fire sprinklers, fire piping systems, fire suppression systems, and firefighting nozzles. Behind them sit the Tyco lines Firepath carries: sprinklers, nozzles and accessories, including Special K ESFR; valves; and corrosion solutions.

Alongside supply, Firepath points customers to Tyco training, certificate issuance, and design resources.
